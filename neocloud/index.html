A note on residency

An EU data centre alone doesn't establish EU data residency.

Where your data is processed matters. So do data transfers, model artefacts, logs, backups, remote administration and the provider's contractual arrangements.

We establish your requirements and design the infrastructure accordingly, working with your legal and compliance teams where interpretation is needed. We won't promise compliance based on a region label.
