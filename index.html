The problem

The demo was the easy part.

In an investment firm, an agent can't simply be given a login and told to get on with it. Someone has to decide what it can see, which actions it can take and what happens when it gets something wrong.

That's engineering work. It needs people who understand production systems and can work with your security, operations and compliance teams without turning the project into a year-long programme.

01

It needs to work with your actual data.

Portfolio records, custodian files, document stores and the applications your team uses every day. Not a clean dataset prepared for a demonstration.

02

Someone has to be accountable.

Which identity does the agent use? Who approves a change? Where does an exception go? Who gets called when the service stops working?

03

Your existing team still has a business to run.

They know your systems. We work with them, rather than asking them to put everything else aside to build an AI platform.

An example of the work

Take the morning reconciliation.

Positions in one system. A custodian statement somewhere else. Your operations team spends the morning finding the differences, checking the source and deciding what needs attention.

An agent could take on the first pass. But getting it into daily use means solving the parts the demo skipped.
