The problem

You need agents to do the work. You cannot afford to get it wrong.

The opportunity is bigger than faster research or better answers. Agents can investigate exceptions, prepare client work, gather evidence and move a process forward. That could change how much work your firm handles without growing headcount at the same rate.

But when software starts taking action, a bad decision can become a bad transaction, an inaccurate report or a client problem. Your firm remains accountable. We help you put agents to work without handing over uncontrolled authority.

03

Get into production without a new programme.

Work with the systems and engineers you already have. Bring in specialist delivery for a defined outcome instead of building and operating everything from scratch.

What an agent doing real work looks like

Imagine an agent handling the first pass of your morning reconciliation.

Positions in one system. A custodian statement somewhere else. Your operations team spends the morning finding the differences, checking the source and deciding what needs attention.

Instead of producing another summary for someone to act on, the agent checks records, investigates breaks and prepares the next step. Your operations team handles exceptions and approves consequential changes. Routine work moves; accountability stays with your firm.

What makes it controlled enough to use?

Agreed authority, reliable source data, tested exceptions, approval for consequential actions, traceable decisions and a clear way to stop or recover when something fails.
