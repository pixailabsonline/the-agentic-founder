Where agents can take real work off the business

Start with the process that's costing you.

Different firms have different bottlenecks. These are examples of work an agent could own from the first request to the point where a person must decide. The right starting point depends on your systems and risk appetite.

01 / Wealth management

Client onboarding that doesn't depend on someone chasing every step.

An agent checks the submitted documents, retrieves approved records, follows up on missing information and prepares the file for the right person to review. Your team handles the decisions that require judgement or regulated approval.

Business case
More clients served without matching growth in administration.
02 / Investment operations

Exceptions investigated before your operations team opens its queue.

An agent compares positions and custodian records, investigates differences against approved sources and prepares the proposed next action. It can carry out actions within agreed limits and escalate the rest, with a record of every step.

Business case
Less manual investigation. More capacity as portfolios grow.
03 / Institutional asset management

Investor requests that move across teams instead of sitting in inboxes.

An agent gathers approved data for due-diligence requests and reporting, checks what's missing, routes questions to the right owner and prepares the response for review. Your people retain control of client commitments and sign-off.

Business case
Respond to more mandates without multiplying the servicing workload.

Illustrative workflows, not claims about completed client projects. We agree each agent's actual authority before deployment.

The part a demo leaves out

An agent that acts needs limits you can live with.

Reading a document is one thing. Updating a record, contacting a client or triggering another system is another. Those actions touch your controls and your reputation.

We work with your operations, technology and risk owners to define what the agent can do. Then we build the integrations, tests and operating controls around those decisions.

Illustrative agent authority

Read approved recordsPermitted
Investigate an exceptionPermitted
Prepare a correctionPermitted
Change a financial recordRequires approval
Act outside agreed scopeBlocked

Identity, scoped permissions, testing, audit records, monitoring and recovery are part of the build.
