Wealth management

A new client says yes. Why does onboarding still take weeks?

Someone checks the documents. Someone else chases a missing form. The same details get entered into another system. An agent could collect what is missing, check the file and get it ready for the person who needs to approve it.

The prizeMore clients through the door without adding the same amount of admin.
Investment operations

Your team starts the morning looking for breaks instead of resolving them.

An agent could compare positions with custodian records, investigate the differences and prepare the next action. Routine fixes can be authorised within agreed limits. Your specialists handle the cases that actually need them.

The prizeMore portfolios handled without a bigger investigation queue.
Asset management

An agent could pull together approved data, chase missing answers internally and prepare the response. Your team checks what goes out and gets back to the investor sooner.

The engineering that makes this possible

A clever demo is easy. Giving an agent access to your business is where the real work starts.

What can it read? What can it change? When does it need a person? What happens when a system is down or the answer is wrong?

That's my work. I build the connections to your existing systems, tie access to your identity controls and test the awkward cases before the agent goes live. Your team can see what it did, stop it and take over when needed.
