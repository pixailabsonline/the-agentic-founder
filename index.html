Where I come in

Giving an agent access is easy. Living with what it does is harder.

Once an agent can change a record or contact a client, the stakes change. Who gave it permission? What did it do? Who can stop it? Those questions need answers before it goes live.

I work with the people who own the process and the systems. We decide exactly what the agent can do, build it into your environment and test what happens when things go wrong.
