## The current positioning

The Agentic Founder provides external agentic AI production capability for UK investment, asset and wealth management firms.

The target firms need to adopt agentic AI but do not have the available team, specialist skills or appetite to build and operate the capability themselves. Their internal technology, data, operations, security and compliance teams are already overloaded. They are accustomed to relying on accountable external suppliers.

The offer is to act as the specialist external team that helps choose, build, launch and operate agentic AI in production. This is about agents acting safely in live operations, not RAG demonstrations, generic AI strategy or another platform sales programme.
